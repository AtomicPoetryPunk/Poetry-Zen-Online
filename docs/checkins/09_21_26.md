    - Default types:
      - Submission / Contest
        - Organization
        - Contest, series, or issue
        - Status (Pending, Accepted, Rejected, Withdrawn)

        - "Needs Acknowledgement?" checkbox

      - Slam Honor
        - Organization
        - Event or series
        - Date
        - Honor (text field)
        - "Needs Acknowledgement?" checkbox
      - Slam Score (not in demo)
        - Organization
        - Event or series
        - Date
        - Score
